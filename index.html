Stack World

Meet Byte. Drive it around an island built from my stack: visit every district, read about my projects, collect all 14 stack orbs, and play the helper bots' minigames.

  • WASD or arrows to move
  • Shift run   Space jump
  • Click or tap the ground to walk there
  • E talk to helper bots and play their minigames
  • M change the mood   V change the camera
  • Scroll or +- to zoom
